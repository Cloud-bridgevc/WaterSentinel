Privacy notice

What this website is

This website describes WaterSentinel and includes an interactive demo that runs on sample data. No customer systems are connected.

Demo data

Changes you make in the demo are kept in your browser (local storage) or in the demo server’s memory and contain only sample data. Clearing site data removes them.

Early-access requests

When the request form is connected to a customer-relationship service (proposed: HubSpot), submitting it sends the fields you enter, your consent choices and the page address to that service so WaterSentinel can reply. Product updates are sent only if you opt in. Until then, requests are kept in your browser and you are offered a pre-filled email to hello@watersentinel.site.

Analytics and cookies

This website includes no advertising trackers. A hosting provider may process routine request data to serve and protect the site.

Your requests

Contact hello@watersentinel.site with questions about information you have sent to WaterSentinel, or to ask for it to be corrected or deleted.
