How it works

From meter reading to closed-out inspection, with the evidence at every step.

01 · INGEST

Connect authorized meters

Interval readings arrive through approved utility, AMI or BMS integrations, or scheduled file drops. Each source is validated and quality-checked on arrival.

02 · DETECT

Flag what breaks pattern

A statistical detector compares every hour with that meter's own hour-of-day and hour-of-week history. Thresholds are yours to tune.

03 · EXPLAIN

Brief the people who go look

The investigation assistant summarises timing, magnitude and night-flow evidence, lists plausible explanations and suggests an inspection sequence.

04 · VERIFY

Inspect, record, learn

Anomalies become assigned inspection tasks. The outcome — verified leak, no leak found, or operational cause — is recorded against the event.

A detected anomaly is…

consumption that differs significantly from what the meter's history predicts. It is a reason to look, ranked by how much it matters.

A verified leak is…

a physical leak located by someone on site. WaterSentinel never labels an event a leak from consumption data alone.

Platform

Consumption anomaly detection

Robust statistics on hourly readings, with configurable thresholds, minimum excess and event grouping per portfolio.

Leak investigation assistant

FAQ

Straight answers.

Does WaterSentinel confirm leaks?

No. It detects consumption that is statistically unusual for that meter and helps you decide where to look. A leak is recorded as verified only after an on-site inspection finds one.

What data do you need?

Interval readings (hourly or finer) from each meter you want monitored, plus basic site context such as floor area and occupancy for comparisons.

Which meters can connect?

We integrate through data sources you are authorized to share: utility or AMI data portals, building management systems, data loggers, or scheduled file exports. Each integration is scoped during onboarding.

How does the AI assistant work?

It receives a structured evidence package computed by the detector and writes a brief from those numbers only. Output is checked so it never claims a leak is confirmed.

Is our data used to train AI models?

No. Briefs are generated with Amazon Bedrock, which does not use customer prompts or outputs to train foundation models. Your data stays in your deployment's AWS region.

Is the console demo live?

The sample console uses fictional sites and simulated readings so you can explore every feature. It is not connected to real meters.
